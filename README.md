## 🙋‍♀️ About Me

🎓 Final-year **BSc in CSE** student at **Southeast University, Dhaka, Bangladesh**

📊 I am building my career in **Data Science, AI and Machine Learning** — working with **Python, NumPy and Matplotlib** to analyse data, visualise patterns and learn how models think

🔬 My final year design project studies how **generative AI** affects students' creativity and academic performance — a questionnaire survey of university students (pilot done with 100 respondents, main survey on the way!)

🌱 Currently learning: **Machine Learning with Python — Pandas, scikit-learn and beyond**

🔌 Side quest: I also build embedded & IoT prototypes (ESP32, Arduino) — smart water tanks and GPS rescue trackers

⚡ Fun fact: I like turning real-life problems — study habits, water tanks, disaster rescue — into data and working prototypes

## 🎯 Current Focus

- 🐍 **Python for Data Science** — NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib
- 🤖 **Machine Learning** — learning the core algorithms step by step
- 📈 **Data Analysis & Visualisation** — surveys, statistics, storytelling with charts
- 🧠 **Generative AI** — how students use it, and what it does to creativity
